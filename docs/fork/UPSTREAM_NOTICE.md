FreeTube v0.25.3-beta (`ff510f8f6c4a0da2602f90336c555f75c75bfa47`). It is not an
official FreeTube release. The FreeTube team and contributors retain authorship
and copyright in their work. The application remains AGPL-3.0-or-later; see
the included LICENSE and upstream attribution throughout the source.

Corresponding source for every DD release is available under the matching tag at
https://github.com/jimbobsmith/drivedeck-desktop. Fork modifications, build scripts
and the complete dependency lockfile are included. Electron and bundled third
party libraries retain their own licenses and notices. Webpack emits bundled
license notices alongside the application JavaScript; Electron distributions
